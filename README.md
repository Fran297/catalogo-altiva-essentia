# Altiva Essentia — Product Photo Source URLs (153 fragrances)

## TL;DR
- The most reliable one-click photo sources are **official brand sites** (lattafa-usa.com, us.afnan.com, armaf.com, bhararabeauty.com, emperperfumes.com) and **FragranceX.com**, which serve clean white-background bottle+box shots you can right-click and save directly; **Fragrantica product pages** are the best universal fallback (one dedicated page per exact flanker) but the site blocks image hotlinking, so you must open the page and save the main bottle image manually.
- I located verified product pages for roughly **130 of the 153 SKUs**; about 18 newer/obscure flankers, the 4 "Kit" gift sets, and the 2 generic accessories have no clean verified single-product photo and are flagged individually with the best available alternative.
- Several list names are mislabeled versions of products actually sold under a different brand or title — **Tiramisu Caramel is a Zimaya scent (not Fragrance World)**, **"Lattafa Erba Pura" does not exist (Erba Pura is Xerjoff; the ME dupe is Maison Alhambra Philos Pura)**, and **"Your Touch" is a Maison Alhambra line** — flagged so you don't upload the wrong bottle.

## Key Findings
- **Fragrantica** has one dedicated page per exact flanker (Khamrah vs Khamrah Dukhan vs Qahwa vs Waha are four separate pages), making it the single most complete source for matching the precise bottle color/design. Its images are white-background and about 375x500px — good, not huge.
- **FragranceX** and the **official brand sites** carry the largest, cleanest images (often 1000px+) and allow direct right-click download; prefer these whenever listed in the Notes column.
- Fragrantica numeric page IDs should be spot-checked before bulk downloading — a wrong ID opens a different perfume. Where I could not verify the exact ID from search results, I point you to the brand's Fragrantica designer page.

- Fragrantica blocks automated image extraction, so direct image (fimgs.net) hotlinks could not be programmatically confirmed; verified product-page URLs are provided instead, which is what a store owner can open and save from.
- Some Fragrantica numeric IDs are drawn from search-result listings rather than a direct page fetch and are marked "verify ID" — spot-check these before bulk use.
- The 4 "Kit" gift sets and 2 generic accessories have no clean standalone product photo and were excluded per your instructions.
- Brand/name mismatches flagged above reflect how these products are actually catalogued at the manufacturer level; the store's internal naming may differ from the true brand, so confirm the physical bottle you stock against the linked image.
- Where an item shares a bottle with another SKU on your list (e.g., Amethyst = Badee Al Oud Amethyst; Sublime = Badee Al Oud Sublime; Oud For Glory = Badee Al Oud For Glory), the same URL is intentionally reused.
